Problem 1: Fourier Analysis
===========================
Definition (Eq. 1):  F(n) = (1/N) * sum_{k=0}^{N-1} f(k) e^{-i2pi nk/N}
                         = (1/N) * sum_k f(k) [cos(2pi nk/N) - i sin(2pi nk/N)]
List L: f(0..5) = 10, 40, 20, 90, 5, 30     N = 6, so the angle step is 2pi/6 = 60 degrees.

Values of cos and sin at multiples of 60 degrees (index m = nk mod 6):
   m :    0      1        2        3      4         5
  cos:    1     1/2     -1/2      -1    -1/2      1/2
  sin:    0   sqrt3/2  sqrt3/2     0  -sqrt3/2  -sqrt3/2      (sqrt3/2 = 0.8660)

  sum f(k)cos = 10(1) + 40(1) + 20(1) + 90(1) + 5(1) + 30(1)
             = 195.0000
  sum f(k)sin = 10(0) + 40(0) + 20(0) + 90(0) + 5(0) + 30(0)

b) Why the maximum frequency cannot exceed 3 = N/2 cycles per scanline
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
With N = 6 samples, a sinusoid needs at least 2 samples per cycle (one for its
peak and one for its trough) to be represented. So at most N/2 = 3 full cycles
fit across the 6 samples. A frequency n > N/2 is sampled too sparsely: its
samples are identical to those of the lower frequency N - n (it "aliases").
For example, frequency n = 4 gives e^{-i2pi 4k/6} = e^{-i2pi(4-6)k/6} = e^{+i2pi 2k/6},
which is just frequency -2, i.e., 2 cycles per scanline traveling the other way.
So frequencies 4 and 5 carry no new information: they are frequencies -2 and -1.
The highest distinct frequency is the Nyquist frequency N/2 = 3.

c) n = 0..5 gives the same results as n = -2..3
-----------------------------------------------
For any integer n and sample k:
    e^{-i2pi (n-6) k / 6} = e^{-i2pi nk/6} * e^{+i2pi k} = e^{-i2pi nk/6} * 1
because e^{i2pi k} = 1 for every integer k. Therefore F(n - 6) = F(n), so:
    F(-2) computed directly from Eq. (1) = 8.7500 - 0.7217i = F(4)
    F(-1) computed directly from Eq. (1) = -9.5833 + 3.6084i = F(5)
    F(0), F(1), F(2), F(3) are the same in both sets.
So {F(-2), F(-1), F(0), F(1), F(2), F(3)} is the same set of numbers as
{F(4), F(5), F(0), F(1), F(2), F(3)}, i.e., the same as n = 0..5. The set
n = -2..3 just relabels them so that no |n| exceeds the 3 cycles/scanline limit.
